# The missing layer

The connected-health stack has become very good at sensing.

Wearables collect sleep, activity, recovery and physiological signals. Interpretation products turn those signals into scores and dashboards. Coaching products provide advice. Habit products help people repeat actions.

The pieces are useful, but the loop is still open.

```text
Sensors        Interpretation       Support          Evidence
measure   →    describes       →    suggests    →    often missing
```

The missing layer is a system that remembers the individual, adapts support to their current state, measures response and carries that evidence forward.

## Trackers measure. Coaches advise. Habit apps nudge.

Opti-U is designed to connect those functions without pretending they are the same thing.

- Measurement is not state estimation.
- Correlation is not causal attribution.
- A confident sentence is not evidence.
- Engagement is not an outcome.
- Population knowledge must not erase individual context.

The category thesis is therefore not “more AI coaching”. It is governed adaptive intelligence above the sensing layer: persistent individual state, structured behavioural support and measured learning in one closed loop.

## Why behavioural and physiological signals belong together

Behaviour does not happen outside the body. The same action may be appropriately challenging on one day and counterproductive on another. Sleep debt, stress, recovery, routine, motivation and context interact.

A useful system needs both sides:

- physiological signals provide state and trajectory context;
- behavioural signals provide intention, adherence and response context;
- structured experiments provide a way to learn rather than assume;
- governance determines when that learning is allowed to influence support.

The aim is not to automate therapy or practise medicine. Opti-U is non-clinical behavioural support. Its job is to help make everyday change more personal, measurable and accessible while preserving clear escalation and care boundaries.

## The product and evidence thesis are the same thesis

If the system cannot show why it adapted, what evidence supported the change and what would make it stop, it has not really learned—it has merely changed.

That is why evidence gates, provenance and safe refusal are product capabilities, not compliance decoration.
