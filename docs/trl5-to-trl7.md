# From TRL5 to TRL7

Building the platform and proving the platform are different programmes.

## Established at TRL5

- integrated operation under controlled representative conditions;
- persistent individual state and personal baselines;
- heterogeneous export-data processing in bounded evidence harnesses;
- conservative suppression under degraded inputs;
- executable forecasting, experimentation and causal-estimation pathways;
- deterministic decision-lineage and governance infrastructure.

## The TRL6 question

Can the integrated system operate safely and usefully with consenting users in a relevant environment?

That requires evidence for:

- provider continuity and data quality over time;
- forecast calibration on live longitudinal data;
- safe degradation and incident handling;
- coaching fidelity and perceived usefulness;
- adherence and completion through a bounded journey;
- consent, withdrawal and erasure in operation;
- no unresolved safety or governance breach.

## The TRL7 question

Can the system operate as a production service with repeatable controls?

That adds:

- multi-user reliability and capacity evidence;
- monitored production operation;
- independent security and governance assurance;
- institutional workflows and support;
- repeatable release, rollback and incident practice;
- behavioural and commercial evidence strong enough for the claims being made.

## The risk-retirement sequence

```text
Freeze the claim
      ↓
Prove the engineering baseline
      ↓
Authorise governance and human oversight
      ↓
Qualify the relevant environment
      ↓
Run a bounded live demonstration
      ↓
Freeze evidence and decide what may now be claimed
```

The programme advances when evidence closes a named risk, not when more features have been added.
